            Every particle is one real colour, taken by k-means clustering from the photograph of one real painting. Nothing here is decorative: no colour was chosen, corrected or invented.

            HOW THE COLOURS WERE MEASURED

              SOURCES

              Images and metadata come from the open-access programmes of these collections, all of them CC0 or public domain. Attribution is on every work: click a particle to see the painting it was measured from and the museum that holds it.

                A handful of paintings are held in more than one of these catalogues. Where the artist, title and date agree, the work is counted once. Five catalogues also spell one painter several ways — Rembrandt three times between them — so spellings are folded to one name per hand, without ever merging an attribution: a follower is not the master.
