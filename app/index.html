Every cell in this field is a real colour, extracted by k-means clustering from the photograph of a real painting. Nothing here is decorative: no colour has been chosen, corrected, or invented. Click any cell to see the work it came from.

METHOD

    WHAT THE GAPS MEAN

    The horizontal axis is ordinal, not linear. Each column holds roughly the same number of paintings, so a column may span a century where the collection is thin and two years where it is dense. The strip at the bottom reports the real span and count of every column. This is a survey of one museum's public-domain holdings, not of art history.

    The field stops around 1910. That is a copyright boundary, not an aesthetic one: paintings after it are almost never in the public domain, so the twentieth century cannot be shown honestly here.
